3. Technical Execution, Waterproofing & Surface Prep Notes

Contractor Guidelines

24" On-Center Framing

Schluter Kerdi-Board (1/2" thickness) is rated for framing spaced up to 24" o.c. Adhere strictly to fastener spacing. If extra backing is required for the 28" horizontal LED niche or valve rough-in, sister 2x4 framing studs have been included.

Oil-Based Paint Adhesion

Painting latex directly over oil base causes peeling. Scuff-sand the walls to dull the sheen, clean dust, and prime with Zinsser B-I-N Shellac-Based Primer to seal old wallpaper paste residue and ensure lasting adhesion.

Tile Leveling System

Use the clip-and-wedge leveling system for the 80 sq. ft. Breeze Blanco III surround to prevent lippage across the 10"x28" horizontal LED niche transitions and corners. Snap clips only after thin-set has fully cured.
